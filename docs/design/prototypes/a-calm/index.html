Research / 01

Algorithmic Advice & Human Decisions

Can an algorithm learn from how people make step-by-step decisions and turn their costliest habit into one short tip they will use?

5 projects · 4 papers · since 2021

In brief

Algorithms can often compute the best next move, yet people ignore or misapply the advice. This direction asks how advice should be framed, as a precise instruction or a broader rule, so that it is adopted, improves decisions now, and still teaches something once the advice is gone.

Research / 02

Generative & Agentic AI at Work

Does generative AI make professionals faster at familiar tasks, or does it change which tasks are worth doing at all?

5 projects · 5 papers · since 2024

In brief

Field data from teachers and other professionals show how generative AI is used in practice. The work asks whether AI mainly speeds up familiar tasks or shifts what people choose to work on, and how advisors and AI agents should share decisions.

Papers with a public version 5

Research / 03

Learning on the Job with Algorithms

Do gig workers get faster and more reliable with experience, and do they rely on the app’s suggestions more or less as they learn?

4 projects · 4 papers · since 2025

In brief

On a platform, the algorithm decides which jobs a worker sees, so it also shapes what they learn. This direction studies how skill builds up on the job and how dispatch and recommendation rules can develop a workforce when workers are free to turn offers down.

Papers with a public version 4

  1. Dispatching to Develop: Worker Agency and Workforce Development in Gig Platforms Working paper (SSRN), 2026
  2. Precise or Broad? The Reward-Learning Frontier in Algorithmic Advice Design Working paper, 2026 Major revision at Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (as reported by the PI, April 2026)
  3. Learning on the Go: Understanding How Gig Economy Workers Learn with Recommendation Algorithms Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction (CSCW 2025), 2025
  4. Learning to Gig: Workforce Development under Worker Agency Working paper (SSRN), 2025 Major revision at Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (as reported by the PI, May 2026)

Research / 04

Gig & Platform Work Design

What gets a ride-hail driver to start or stop a shift: the pay rate, an earnings goal, or the momentum of hours already worked?

5 projects · 6 papers · since 2021

In brief

Gig workers choose when, where and for which platform to work. Using large-scale platform data, this direction studies what drives those choices and which pay designs (guarantees, streak bonuses, wage floors) change how much people work and how often they switch apps.

Papers with a public version 6

  1. Dispatching to Develop: Worker Agency and Workforce Development in Gig Platforms Working paper (SSRN), 2026
  2. Managing Multihoming Workers in the Gig Economy Working paper (SSRN), 2026 Revise and resubmit at Operations Research (as reported in the PI's CV, Aug 2026)
