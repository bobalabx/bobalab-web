Algorithmic Advice & Human Decisions

Guiding question

When should advice name the exact next move, and when should it teach a broader rule that people can carry into new situations?

5 projects · 4 papers

Why it matters

Algorithms can often work out a better course of action than people find on their own, but advice only helps if people act on it. This direction asks how advice should be shaped and how precise it should be, so that it improves decisions in the moment and still leaves people more capable once the algorithm steps away.

Generative & Agentic AI at Work

Guiding question

Does generative AI raise productivity by speeding up familiar tasks, or by changing which tasks are worth taking on?

5 projects · 5 papers

Why it matters

Generative AI and AI agents are entering everyday professional work, from lesson planning to service workflows. This direction looks at how people actually use these tools on real tasks, what that changes about their work, and how much authority an AI agent should be given.

Learning on the Job with Algorithms

Guiding question

Do gig workers get better with experience, and do they rely on the app’s recommendations more or less as they learn?

4 projects · 4 papers

Why it matters

On work platforms, the app often decides what job a person sees next, so recommendation and dispatch systems shape how workers learn. This direction studies how skill builds alongside algorithms, and how platforms could assign work so that people improve rather than only cover today’s demand.

Gig & Platform Work Design

Guiding question

When drivers split their hours across rival apps, which pay designs change how much they drive and how often they switch?

5 projects · 5 papers

Why it matters

Flexible platform work lets people decide when and where to work, which puts their choices at the centre of how platforms run. This direction studies what drives those choices, and how pay, incentives and staffing can be designed for workers who always have other options.
