## Redesign copy changes: PI to approve

**Prepared by:** the redesign CONTENT package, 2026-10-24, on branch `redesign` (DECISIONS D-13 item 7; AUDIT §3.5 W1–W5, L3). An adversarial review the same day rewrote R-2, R-9, R-13 and R-14, corrected two more statements (R-18, R-19), added one question (R-20) and fixed the word counts, which now follow the site validator's rule (`W_BODY_LENGTH`, 80–150 words for live directions).

**What changed and why.** The audit found that the most "machine-written" feature of the site was its copy.
