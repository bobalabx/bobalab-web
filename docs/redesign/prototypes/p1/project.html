Project

Learning on the Job

Dispatching for Worker Development

Algorithms for offering jobs to gig workers who can decline and who learn at different, unknown rates, so that platforms build skill as well as fill orders.

Directions
Learning on the Job, Platform Work
Papers
2
Team
William Deng, Shunan Jiang, Park Sinchaisri

The problem

How should a platform offer jobs when its workers can turn offers down, and get better at the work at different rates the platform cannot see in advance?

Asks how a platform should offer jobs when workers may decline and improve at different, unknown rates. Proposes a tractable benchmark and learning algorithms, including a variant that caps the risk of market failure, and tests them on delivery-platform data.

The companion paper, Dispatching to Develop, studies dispatch policies that develop gig workers’ skills.
