Mark sheet

The ruled b

Every figure on this site opens with a 2 px ink rule. The mark is that rule set over the lab’s initial, drawn from STIX Two Text Bold. It is a letterform, so it does not compete with the circles that interface icons already own, and it carries no pearl substitution.

Rejected, and why

  • Flat pearl. Reads as a full stop or bullet at 16 px.
  • Pearl, orbit, seeds. Reads as a radio button or an orbit icon at 16 px.
  • The ruled b. Kept: no circle, a reason (the figure rule), and it holds at 16 px.
