The rule

A position above the floor may be filled only when both positions beneath it are filled. The four-row frame allows exactly 41 settled stacks; four of them are the direction glyphs. The same rule drops each paper into Figure 1: the lowest settled slot, leftmost first, in order of year. Work in progress is drawn as open rings above the pile, because it has not settled yet.
