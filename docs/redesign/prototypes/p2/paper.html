Improving Human Sequential Decision Making with Reinforcement Learning

Hamsa Bastani, Osbert Bastani and Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri

Management Science 72(1), 733–755, 2026. Published online 22 May 2025.

Journal article

  • Algorithmic Advice

PDFarXivPublisherDOI

In brief

An algorithm learns from logs of people's multi-step decisions and turns the biggest gap between their play and an optimal policy into one short tip. In virtual-kitchen experiments the tips raised performance, and people combined them with their own strategies.
