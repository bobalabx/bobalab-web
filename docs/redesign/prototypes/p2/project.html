Dispatching for Worker Development

Algorithms for offering jobs to gig workers who can decline and who learn at different, unknown rates, so that platforms build skill as well as fill orders.

  • Learning on the Job
  • Platform Work

2 papers, 3 people.

The question

How should a platform offer jobs when workers may decline and improve at different, unknown rates?

Gig platforms usually dispatch to cover today’s demand. This project treats each offer as a chance to build skill as well, for workers who can say no and who learn at rates the platform cannot see in advance.

Approach

Learning to Gig proposes a tractable benchmark and learning algorithms, including a variant that caps the risk of market failure, and tests them on delivery-platform data. A second working paper, Dispatching to Develop, studies dispatch policies that develop gig workers’ skills.
