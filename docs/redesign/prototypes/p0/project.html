Dispatching for Worker Development

Algorithms for offering jobs to gig workers who can decline and who learn at different, unknown rates, so that platforms build skill as well as fill orders.

Directions
  • Learning on the Job
  • Platform Work
People
3 lab members
Papers
2 working papers
Latest
Major revision at M&SOM, May 2026

The question

How should a platform offer jobs when workers may decline and improve at different, unknown rates?

Adapted from the summary of Learning to Gig (Deng, Jiang, Qu and Sinchaisri, 2025).

The paper proposes a tractable benchmark and learning algorithms, including a variant that caps the risk of market failure, and tests them on delivery-platform data. A second working paper, Dispatching to Develop, continues the work on dispatch policies that develop gig workers’ skills.
