Learning on the Job and Platform Work

Dispatching for Worker Development

Algorithms for offering jobs to gig workers who can decline and who learn at different, unknown rates, so that platforms build skill as well as fill orders.

The question

How should a platform offer jobs when workers may decline and improve at different, unknown rates?

Learning to Gig proposes a tractable benchmark and learning algorithms, including a variant that caps the risk of market failure, and tests them on delivery-platform data. It received a major revision from Manufacturing & Service Operations Management in May 2026. Dispatching to Develop, posted in August 2026, studies dispatch policies that develop gig workers’ skills.
