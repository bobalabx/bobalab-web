  1. Managing Multihoming Workers in the Gig Economy

    Gad Allon, Maxime C. Cohen, Ken Moon, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri

    SSRN working paper 4502968. Revise and resubmit at Operations Research

    Combines one ride-hailing platform's data with public New York City trip data to estimate how drivers split time between competing apps, then tests pay designs: guaranteed hourly pay can raise supply at fixed cost, streak bonuses curb switching, and wage floors need limits on congestion.

    Working paperPlatform Work1 awardPDFSSRNDOI

  2. Improving Human Sequential Decision Making with Reinforcement Learning

    Hamsa Bastani, Osbert Bastani, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri

    Management Science 72(1), 733–755

    An algorithm learns from logs of people's multi-step decisions and turns the biggest gap between their play and an optimal policy into one short tip. In virtual-kitchen experiments the tips raised performance, and people combined them with their own strategies.

    Journal articleAlgorithmic Advice7 awardsPDFarXivPublisherDOI

  3. Precise or Broad? The Reward-Learning Frontier in Algorithmic Advice Design

    Philippe Blaettchen, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri

    Major revision at Manufacturing & Service Operations Management

    Compares two ways of turning an optimal policy into advice in EV-charging route experiments: an exact action versus a broader rule of thumb. Exact advice performs best while it is visible; broader advice can leave people better able to act alone in similar settings once advice is removed.

    Working paperAlgorithmic AdviceLearning on the JobPDF

  6. The Teacher’s Knapsack: Generative AI and Teacher Productivity

    Samantha Keppler, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri, Clare Snyder

    SSRN working paper 4924786

    Frames lesson preparation as picking the quickest set of tasks that meets a learning goal. A year-long case study of 24 teachers suggests generative AI helps both by speeding familiar tasks and by making it worthwhile to switch to new ones, though switching costs hold some teachers back.

    Working paperAI at WorkPDFSSRNDOI

  7. Strategic Advice in the Age of Personal AI

    Yueyang Liu, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri

    arXiv preprint, v2

    A game-theoretic model of a professional advisor whose client may also consult a personal AI. The advisor tilts advice to offset the AI, and is worst off when clients use the AI some of the time rather than never or always.

    Working paperAI at WorkAlgorithmic AdvicearXivDOI

  1. Learning to Gig: Workforce Development under Worker Agency

    Haonan Deng, Shunan Jiang, Xinpeng Qu, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri

    SSRN working paper 5387320. Major revision at Manufacturing & Service Operations Management

    Asks how a platform should offer jobs when workers may decline and improve at different, unknown rates. Proposes a tractable benchmark and learning algorithms, including a variant that caps the risk of market failure, and tests them on delivery-platform data.

    Working paperLearning on the JobPlatform WorkSSRNDOI

  2. Learning on the Go: Understanding How Gig Economy Workers Learn with Recommendation Algorithms

    Shunan Jiang, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri

    Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction 9(7), 1–35

    Draws on more than a million delivery orders to show couriers get faster and more punctual with experience. Newcomers tend to follow the app's job recommendations, while seasoned workers increasingly go their own way.

    Journal articleLearning on the JobPlatform WorkPDFSSRNPublisherDOI

  3. Making ChatGPT Work for Me

    Samantha Keppler, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri, Clare Snyder

    Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction 9(2), 1–23

    Observes 24 K-12 teachers using ChatGPT on their own work and sorts their requests into a handful of distinct ways of asking for help; most conversations stay within one of them.

    Journal articleAI at WorkPDFSSRNPublisherDOI

  4. Understanding Non-Adoption of “Optimal” Algorithmic Advice for Problem Solving

    David Lee, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri

    Presented at the CHI 2022 Workshop on Trust and Reliance in AI-Human Teams

    Looks at why people in the virtual-kitchen task pass over algorithm-generated tips, finding they interpret tips differently and face barriers beyond trust, such as how hard a tip is to apply and features of the task setting.

    Working paperAlgorithmic Advice

  1. The Impact of Behavioral and Economic Drivers on Gig Economy Workers

    Gad Allon, Maxime C. Cohen, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri

    Manufacturing & Service Operations Management 25(4), 1376–1393

    Uses ride-hailing data to model when on-demand drivers start and stop working. Pay matters, but so do habits such as quitting after reaching an earnings goal and staying on once a shift has momentum; incentives that account for both can add capacity without extra cost.

    Journal articlePlatform Work4 awardsPDFSSRNPublisherDOI

    Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri, Shane T. Jensen

    PLOS ONE 16(12), e0257530

    Turns Philadelphia block-party permits into two measures of neighborhood ‘vibrancy’ and links them to crime: more parties overall go with more crime, but a larger share of spontaneous parties goes with lower and falling crime.

    Rim Hariss, Georgia Perakis, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri, Yanchong (Karen) Zheng
