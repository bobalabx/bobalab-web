How people learn to work with algorithms

Berkeley Operations and
Behavioral Analytics Lab
Haas School of Business
University of California, Berkeley

We study how people learn, decide and work alongside algorithms and AI, with field data from gig platforms and controlled experiments. The work runs along four lines: Algorithmic Advice, AI at Work, Learning on the Job and Platform Work.

  3. Strategic Advice in the Age of Personal AI

    Yueyang Liu, Wichinpong Park Sinchaisri

    arXiv preprint, v2

    A game-theoretic model of a professional advisor whose client may also consult a personal AI. The advisor tilts advice to offset the AI, and is worst off when clients use the AI some of the time rather than never or always.
