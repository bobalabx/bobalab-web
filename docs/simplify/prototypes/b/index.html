BOBALAB is the Berkeley Operations and Behavioral Analytics Lab, Prof. Park Sinchaisri’s research group at the Haas School of Business, UC Berkeley.

We study how people learn to work with algorithms.

Apps now assign gig work, algorithms hand out advice, and AI sits beside teachers as they plan lessons. With field data and experiments, we look at how people actually learn, decide and work alongside these systems, and how to design them so they serve the people who use them.

Inside a study

Can one short tip make people better at a hard, multi-step job?

An algorithm reads logs of people’s step-by-step choices in a virtual kitchen and turns the biggest gap between their play and an optimal policy into one short tip. In experiments, the tips raised performance, and people combined them with their own strategies.

Hamsa Bastani, Osbert Bastani and Park Sinchaisri, “Improving Human Sequential Decision Making with Reinforcement Learning,” Management Science 72(1), 2026.
