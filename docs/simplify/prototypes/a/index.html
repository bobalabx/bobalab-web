How people learn to work with algorithms

BOBALAB is Prof. Park Sinchaisri’s research lab at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business. We study how people learn, decide and work alongside algorithms and AI, from gig drivers to teachers, to improve how people and AI work together.

Research directions

  • Algorithmic AdviceHow algorithms should advise people When should advice name the exact next move, and when should it give a broader rule people can carry into new situations? 4 papers, 2 in progress.
  • AI at WorkHow professionals really use AI What kinds of help do teachers ask ChatGPT for on their own real tasks, and do their conversations stick to one kind? 5 papers, 2 in progress.
  • Learning on the JobHow workers learn alongside apps Do gig workers get faster and more reliable with experience, and do they lean on or drift away from the app’s recommendations as they learn? 4 papers, 1 in progress.
  • Platform WorkWhen gig workers work, and how they are paid What drives a ride-hail driver’s choice to log on and log off: the pay rate, an earnings target, or the momentum of a long shift? 6 papers, 2 in progress.

Inside a study

Turning decision data into one short tip

In a virtual kitchen, players decide who chops, cooks and plates. An algorithm reads the logs of their choices and turns the biggest gap between their play and an optimal policy into a single tip. In experiments the tips raised performance, and people combined them with their own strategies.

Bastani, Bastani & Sinchaisri, Management Science, 2026. Seven awards, 2021 to 2023.
