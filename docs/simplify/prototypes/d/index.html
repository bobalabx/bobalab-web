How people learn to work with algorithms

BOBALAB is a research lab at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business, led by Professor Park Sinchaisri. Apps and AI now hand out gig work, give advice and help with everyday tasks. We study how people learn, decide and work alongside them, using field data and experiments, to improve human–AI interaction in the future of work.

What we study

  1. When should advice name the exact next move, and when should it give a broader rule people can carry into new situations?

    One finding: Short tips, distilled by reinforcement learning from people’s own decisions, raised performance in virtual-kitchen experiments, and people combined them with their own strategies. Bastani, Bastani & Sinchaisri, Management Science

    4 papers, 2 in progress. With William Deng and Park Sinchaisri. Explore Algorithmic Advice

  2. What kinds of help do teachers ask ChatGPT for on their own real tasks, and do their conversations stick to one kind?

    One finding: Watching 24 K-12 teachers use ChatGPT on their own work, the study found a handful of distinct ways of asking for help; most conversations stay within one of them. Keppler, Sinchaisri & Snyder, 2025

    5 papers, 2 in progress. With William Deng and Park Sinchaisri. Explore AI at Work

  3. Do gig workers get faster and more reliable with experience, and do they lean on or drift away from the app’s recommendations as they learn?

    One finding: Across more than a million delivery orders, couriers got faster and more punctual with experience. Newcomers tend to follow the app’s job recommendations; seasoned workers increasingly go their own way. Jiang & Sinchaisri, 2025

    4 papers, 1 in progress. With Ryan Jesubalan, William Deng and Park Sinchaisri. Explore Learning on the Job

  4. What drives a ride-hail driver’s choice to log on and log off: the pay rate, an earnings target, or the momentum of a long shift?

    One finding: Pay matters, but so do habits: drivers tend to stop after reaching an earnings goal and to stay on once a shift has momentum. Allon, Cohen & Sinchaisri, M&SOM 2023

    6 papers, 2 in progress. With William Deng and Park Sinchaisri. Explore Platform Work
