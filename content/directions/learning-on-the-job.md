So far most of this work comes from gig and delivery platforms. Using more than a million delivery orders, we follow how couriers' speed and punctuality change with experience and how their use of the app's job recommendations shifts. We also ask how a platform should offer jobs when workers can turn offers down and improve at rates the platform can't see in advance, and we test learning algorithms for this on delivery-platform data.

_Dispatching to Develop_ (SSRN, 2026) is our newest paper on this question, and a new project asks whether skill learned in one job carries over to another.
