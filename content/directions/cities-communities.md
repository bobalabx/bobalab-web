In a 2021 PLOS ONE paper, Park Sinchaisri and Shane Jensen use block-party permits issued in Philadelphia to build two measures of neighborhood vibrancy: how many gatherings a neighborhood holds, and what share of them are spontaneous. They then relate these measures to crime across the city's neighborhoods over time.

The study predates the lab; it comes from Park Sinchaisri's doctoral work at the University of Pennsylvania.
