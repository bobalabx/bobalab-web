Some shoppers read a higher price as a sign of higher quality and react to the size of a discount compared with what they expected. Our markdown-pricing paper builds this behavior into a retailer's markdown plan and calibrates the model with a consumer study. It grew out of Park Sinchaisri's 2016 MIT master's thesis on pricing with quality perception, which pairs a model with an experiment.

More recent work uses generative-AI simulation, guided by economic mechanisms, for pricing operations (a paper now under submission). Another project, _Experimentation without Experiments_, asks how firms can learn about pricing without running costly live experiments.
