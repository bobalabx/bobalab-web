Much of this work starts with teachers. We followed K-12 teachers as they used ChatGPT on their own tasks and sorted the kinds of help they asked for, and a year-long case study looks at how generative AI changes their productivity and the tasks they choose. We also model how a professional advisor responds when clients can consult a personal AI.

Newer work looks at where an AI assistant's memory of its user breaks down, when an AI agent in a service workflow should act, ask or defer, and how AI changes which tasks people plan and which they carry out.
