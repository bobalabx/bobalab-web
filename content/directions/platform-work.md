Much of this line is about ride-hailing. We model when drivers start and stop working, looking at pay alongside habits such as earnings targets and the momentum of a long shift. With one platform's records and public New York City trip data, we also estimate how drivers split their time between competing apps and compare pay designs such as hourly guarantees, streak bonuses and wage floors.

Current projects look at how to design and sequence incentives for platform workers and how to staff fluid teams in a fragmented labor market. The line goes back to Park Sinchaisri's 2021 doctoral dissertation at the University of Pennsylvania, on managing the gig economy.
